# Paragon Global Training (PGT) Website Redesign & Implementation Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
This document serves as the master blueprint for the complete redesign and architectural restructuring of the Paragon Global Training (PGT) website. The strategic goal is to transform PGT from a standard "training provider" into a **premium European institute** for Executive Success and Professional Excellence. 

The strategy weaves together two critical dimensions:
1. **The Global Design DNA (Look and Feel):** A premium, timeless aesthetic ("Harvard Business School meets Apple meets McKinsey") emphasizing trust, clarity, and authority.
2. **The New Website Architecture:** A completely overhauled 8-phase page structure that focuses on outcomes and strategic partnerships, moving away from simple course catalogues.

*Note: As per requirements, the Sales Funnel and Knowledge Ecosystem advanced tiering have been excluded from this iteration. This plan focuses strictly on the core redesign and the 8-phase content architecture.*

## 2. Global Design DNA (Look & Feel)

### 2.1. Design Philosophy & Emotional Journey
The website is designed to lead the visitor through a deliberate sequence of emotions without ever feeling like a direct sales pitch. 
The emotional journey must be:
1. **First Impression:** "I've come to the right place." (Instant credibility)
2. **Second Impression:** "These people clearly know what they're doing." (Demonstrated expertise)
3. **Third Impression:** "They understand organisations like mine." (Deep empathy and relevance)
4. **Fourth Impression:** "I want to work with them." (Desire for partnership)

The overarching aesthetic is **The Editorial Institute**: It must read and feel like a premium international publication (e.g., *Harvard Business Review*, *MIT Sloan Management Review*, *McKinsey Insights*). It is not flashy, not corporate, and not academic. It is Premium, Timeless, and Confident.

### 2.2. Core Design Principles
- **Space is Luxury:** Generous whitespace is mandatory. Avoid overcrowding. Apple, McKinsey, and Harvard utilize space to project confidence.
- **Typography:** A single, elegant sans-serif font family must be used globally. Allowed options include Inter, IBM Plex Sans, Manrope, Neue Haas Grotesk, or Avenir Next. The layout should feature large editorial headlines, short paragraphs, and comfortable reading grids. Nothing decorative.
- **Photography Rules:** 
  - **Strict Prohibition:** NO generic stock photos of people shaking hands, NO fake business meetings, NO smiling people pointing at laptops, NO robots or holograms for AI.
  - **Executive School:** Authentic boardrooms, strategy sessions, executives in deep discussion, decision-making environments, modern architecture.
  - **Academic School:** Universities, innovation labs, authentic student collaboration, researchers, technology integration, real learning.
  - **AI:** Minimalist screens, data visualizations, future-focused technology abstractions.
- **Colour Palette:** Disciplined and purposeful. Avoid introducing many additional colors.
  - **Primary:** Deep Navy (Charcoal Blue) - Represents Authority, Trust, Executive presence.
  - **Secondary:** Warm White - Creates space, luxury, clean reading.
  - **Accent:** Gold or Copper (Subtle metallic tone, NEVER bright yellow) - Used sparingly for buttons, icons, highlights, and key numbers. Never for large areas.
  - **Supporting:** Slate Grey, Soft Blue Grey - Used very lightly for neutral backgrounds.
- **UI Components:**
  - *Buttons:* Simple, rounded, strictly using the accent color. Must be large enough to tap comfortably. Primary: "Book a Consultation". Secondary: "Explore Programmes". Avoid having multiple button styles.
  - *Cards:* Rounded corners, subtle shadows, plenty of spacing. Never overcrowded. Every card should feel like a premium publication rather than a software dashboard.
  - *Icons:* Avoid colorful illustrations. Use a single thin-line icon family with consistent sizing. Strict and professional.
  - *Statistics:* Big typography with minimal explanation. E.g., "25+" instead of "25 Years"; "6,000+" instead of "6000 Students".
  - *Animations:* Very subtle fades, slides, and hovers. Nothing spinning, bouncing, or distracting. Luxury brands don't shout.
- **Mobile Experience:** Crucial for CEOs and executives checking from their phones. Requires large typography, short sections, full-width buttons, plenty of spacing, fast loading, and minimal scrolling fatigue.

### 2.3. Section-Specific Design Themes
- **Hero Sections:** Cinematic openings instead of generic banners. 
  - *Executive School:* Panoramic view of a boardroom overlooking a city skyline. Minimal text, strong typography, one CTA.
  - *Academic Excellence:* University campus, researchers collaborating, students working. Bright, open, optimistic.
  - *Knowledge Hub:* Books, digital screens, innovation. Quiet confidence.
- **The Executive School Page:** Must feel distinctly darker, more premium, like an exclusive executive club. Colors: Black, deep navy, glass, warm lighting.
- **Academic School Page:** Much brighter, open, modern, innovative, and inspirational while remaining premium.

## 4. Technical Implementation & Development Roadmap

### Phase A: Design System & UI Architecture
1. **Theme Configuration:** Update `tailwind.config.ts` to strictly enforce the Navy, Warm White, and Gold color palette. Remove unused or clashing colors.
2. **Typography Integration:** Setup the chosen primary Sans-Serif font across the entire Next.js application globally. Ensure robust heading hierarchies (`h1` through `h6`) for editorial styling.
3. **Component Refactoring:** Rebuild base UI components (`button.tsx`, `card.tsx`) to match the "Space is Luxury" and "Premium Publication" guidelines. Ensure generous padding and subtle shadows.

### Phase B: Core Content Pages (Phases 1-4, 7-8)
1. **Homepage:** Build the cinematic hero section, trust bar, and the dual-school visual cards.
2. **About PGT & Contact:** Implement the narrative sections for About. For Contact, build the categorized enquiry router (rather than a simple form) and integrate Calendly. Implement the "Not Sure Where to Begin?" outcome-focused prompt.
3. **School Pages:**
   - *Executive Success:* Implement the dark theme context. Build the interactive 9-pillar wheel component for the Executive Success Model™. Create the horizontal ecosystem timeline.
   - *Academic & Workforce:* Implement the bright, open theme and the Future-Ready framework visual layout.
4. **The PGT Journal:** Build the editorial layout for articles, featuring the Hero Article banner, topic filters, and visual trending cards.

### Phase C: Interactive Logic & Knowledge Hub (Phases 5-6)
1. **Learning Pathways (Phase 5):** 
   - Build the 6 pathway cards.
   - **Develop the Interactive Programme Finder Widget:** Create a multi-step React component with state management to capture user inputs (Who are you?, Main objective?, Outcome?) and render a dynamic recommendation with a consultation CTA.
2. **Knowledge Hub (Phase 6):**
   - Build the resource grid with category filtering.
   - Implement the lead capture forms (email collection) for downloading Tier 1 resources.
   - Connect lead capture forms to the email marketing/CRM platform.
