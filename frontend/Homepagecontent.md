# PG TRAINING

## Training That Transforms Careers, Institutions, and Lives

### Empowering Professionals for More Than 25 Years

For over two and a half decades, PG Training has been helping professionals unlock their potential, strengthen their capabilities, and achieve meaningful growth. As the dedicated training division of Paragon Global Internships (PGI), we have successfully supported more than 6,000 professionals from universities, educational institutions, public organisations, and private sector environments.

Our mission extends beyond traditional training. We believe that meaningful learning creates lasting transformation—not only in careers, but also in confidence, resilience, leadership ability, and personal development.

Through our extensive portfolio of bespoke programmes, we help academics, researchers, administrators, managers, directors, and emerging leaders develop the skills necessary to thrive in an increasingly complex and competitive world.

Many of our programmes are Erasmus+ compatible, enabling institutions and individuals to access valuable European funding opportunities while achieving internationally recognised learning outcomes.
