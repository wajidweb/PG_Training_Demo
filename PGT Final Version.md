My Final Strategic Recommendation
After working through all eight phases, I think we've moved beyond creating a conventional training website. What we're designing is the digital presence of an institute with a clear point of view and two recognised centres of excellence.
To reinforce that positioning, I'd introduce one final element on the Contact page:
"Not Sure Where to Begin?"
Instead of asking visitors to decide which service they need, invite them to describe what they're trying to achieve.
For example:
Tell us about your organisation and your objectives. We'll recommend the most appropriate learning pathway—whether that's executive development, workforce capability, AI adoption, Erasmus+ or a bespoke solution.
This subtle shift changes the conversation from "Which course do you want?" to "What outcome are you trying to achieve?"
That is exactly how trusted advisers, executive education institutes and top consulting firms engage with clients. It reinforces PGT's role as a strategic partner focused on achieving results, not simply delivering training.
